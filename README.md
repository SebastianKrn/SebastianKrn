# Sebastian Kern

Co-founder and CPO of the [Stoicera Software Group](https://stoicera.com), Upper Austria. Product engineer: I decide, specify, sell and check. AI agents build, test, deploy and operate.

## What I build

- Web applications, applied AI and EU-hosted infrastructure for companies in Austria and the DACH region. Next.js, TypeScript, Python, Java and Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Hetzner.
- Products: [MealTime](https://schulmittag.at) (meal registration for schools and municipalities), [einvoice-at](https://github.com/Stoicera/einvoice_at) (Austrian e-invoicing, open source), [migration-lab](https://github.com/Stoicera/migration-lab-java) (a legacy modernisation done in public), [Stoicera Bid Desk](https://biddesk.stoicera.com) (tender screening, pilot), KFZ Digital (inquiry triage for car dealers, pilot).
- Contract work on the Microsoft and Java stacks runs under our sub-brand [Lugmayr-Kern](https://lugmayrkern.at).

## How I work

Two founders, no employees, no outside capital. A narrow stack, decisions within a day, work shipped in small pieces every day. Every claim carries a date and every number a source, including the ones that did not flatter us: the AI test-generation experiment in migration-lab is published with its first, too-good result and the correction. Ship early, measure, iterate.

## Now (October 2026)

- Getting einvoice-at ready for its open-source launch. v0.2.0 shipped on 9 October 2026.
- Running two pilots that triage written requests with a decision model behind a swappable interface: Bid Desk for public tenders, KFZ Digital for dealer inquiries. Unsure means a human decides; a human always sends.
